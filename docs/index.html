Trio in sync

The three stocks are one system. When the trio as a whole is above its 10-day average, every level trade gets a bigger slot; below it, a smaller one. A stock that dips while its two partners rise gets more; one that dips with its partners all selling off gets less. Never zero: the bot always keeps trading. (Tested: better in 4 of 4 tune/test checks across two trios.)
