Uranium and the fuel chain

Spot has climbed from $84.18 in May to $91.55 in October. Long-term contract price is at $98, a record in our data, and spot sits $6.45 below it. Steady contract prices suggest utilities are still buying, but the 20% discount on the Sprott trust (NAV $21.60 vs price $17.22) means it cannot raise money to buy more uranium. Prices are well below the $136 all-time high.

  • Energy Fuels: news of a $725M US loan pact for rare earth production and a target of 1M lb of uranium at White Mesa. The rare earth loan is not uranium demand.
  • Denison and other miners had no hard catalyst in our data.

Plants and power

US fleet is 89% running. It is refueling season, so planned outages are routine. CEG's own fleet is 95% running; VST and TLN are 100%.

  • CEG: a director bought $418K in August at $278.62, a meaningful open-market buy. No insider sales in 12 months.
  • TLN: COO sold about $19.8M on Nov 17 (price range $345-365). We have no evidence it was planned, so we treat it as a mild negative. Net insider selling is $23.4M over 12 months.
  • CEG's past deal headlines were followed by a median -6.0% over 5 days, so contract news has not been a buy signal.
